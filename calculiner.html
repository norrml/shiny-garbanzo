CALCULINER

What Calculiner does (click to fold)
  • Live results. Type any expression and the answer appears greyed out on the same line (=8). Spacing doesn't matter. Each Enter-line is its own calculation, and rows are shaded alternately. Long lines use the full box width before wrapping.
  • Colours. Green = a date word or date result the app recognised. Blue = a locked number result. Red = a variable (its definition and wherever you use it). Grey = live results, suggestions and notes. Light grey = text the app detected and ignored (comments).
  • Operators. + - * / ÷ ( ) %. Multiplication is * only (the × sign is not supported). Percent is relative: 200+10% = 220, 200-10% = 180, and a lone 50% = 0.5.
  • Factorials. Off by default. Tick "Factorials (!)" to allow 5! (whole numbers 0 to 170). While off, a line containing 5! shows no result and a grey note explains why. Factorials work with separators, locking and variables like any other calculation.
  • Several calculations on one line. A ; anywhere starts a new, independent calculation, and typing it locks the result before it: 3+4;5*2 becomes 3+4=7;5*2 with a grey result for the last one. A space after a locked result also starts a new calculation: 3+4=7 5*2. A space after a result followed by an operator is still a chain (=7 +5); to start a calculation with a minus sign, use ;. A space is only a separator after a locked result; 5 6 is not two calculations. Only the last calculation on a line shows a live grey result. You can define variables after a ;: 3+4; v=5.
  • Comments. Words the app ignores (like rent in rent 500 + 200), words with digits in them (v99t), and ignored variable names turn light grey. A line starting with # is ignored completely. A # in the middle of a line is just ignored punctuation.
  • Dates. Use today, tomorrow, yesterday, weekday names, or a date with a month name: 26 oct, 26 oct 2026, oct 26, 26th October (no year = this year). Weekdays: friday = the first Friday after today; next friday = Friday of next week (weeks run Monday to Sunday); last friday = the most recent Friday before today. Add or subtract whole days, weeks, months, years: today + 2 weeks, tomorrow - 3 days, next friday + 1 month. Two dates subtracted give the days between. A weekday written next to a full date (Fri 6 Nov) is just a label. Month maths stops at month end (31 Jan + 1 month = 28 Feb). Numeric dates like 26/10/2026 are not supported. Dates can't be stored in variables.
  • Leaving the page open. The app checks the date every 30 seconds, and when you return to the tab. If the day changes, results that use today, tomorrow, yesterday or weekday names are recalculated automatically (locked ones too) and a banner tells you. Dates you typed out in full never change. The toolbar shows the date the app is using.
  • Autocomplete. When you type 2+ letters at the end of a line, the rest appears in grey: today, tomorrow, yesterday, weekdays, months, and your own variable names (date words are checked first). Press Tab or → to accept. After a number it suggests days, weeks, months, years instead (mo becomes months, not Monday). After a locked result and a space, you're starting a new calculation, so normal suggestions apply. While a suggestion shows, the result on that line is hidden and Enter makes a plain new line.
  • Unfinished input. 39+1+ still shows =40, and open brackets are closed for you.
  • Locking a result. Type =, press Tab (when no suggestion is showing), or press Enter. The answer turns blue (or green for dates). Enter also starts a new line, but only if the line has something to calculate. Shift+Enter always gives a plain new line. = and Tab are ignored when there is nothing new to lock (right after a result, on an empty line, or right after ;), so you can't duplicate a result. Keep typing an operator after a locked result to continue from it (today= then + 3 days). Edit anything earlier and the locked results update. Backspace removes the = and its result together. A digit can't be typed straight after a locked result; type a space or an operator first.
  • Variables. Type name=number (e.g. v=1.5) and it turns red; later uses of the name are red too. Names are letters only, not case-sensitive, and whole-word: v and vv are different variables. Several can share a line: v=1 rr=2 dd=4 or v=1 ; rr=2. Variables work on every line, above or below where they're defined. 2v multiplies (=3), 1+v = 2.5, and today + n days works. If a name is defined twice, the lowest definition wins. Names containing digits (v99t=3) are treated as comments. A definition must come first in its line or after a ;.
  • Reserved words can't be variables. A variable with one of these names is ignored: a grey note appears on the line and it shows dimmed in the variable list. (Typing today= or sun= locks the date instead, since = means lock.) Reserved:
  • Variable list. Every detected variable and its value is shown under the box. A name defined more than once is flagged in red with the line that wins. Ignored names appear dimmed.
  • Saving. Everything you type and your settings are saved automatically in this browser (the toolbar shows when). It is not a backup: clearing browser data, a private window, or moving the file (in some browsers) loses it. Clear deletes the sheet and its saved copy after asking.
  • Popup window. "Open in popup" opens Calculiner in its own small window. It can't stay on top of other windows (browsers don't allow that). The popup and the main window share the same saved sheet and stay in sync; type in one at a time.
  • Comments in calculations. Letters, commas and other punctuation are skipped, so rent 500 + food 200 gives 700. Careful: a comment word that matches a defined variable is read as that variable. A weekday word (sat, sun, wed, mon ...) anywhere is read as a date, and a month word next to a day number (mar 5) is too. A 4-digit number right after a day and month is read as the year.
  • Rounding. Results are rounded to 2 decimals by default (choose 0 to 5). Each locked result carries forward exactly as shown.
  • Commas in results. Off by default. Choose International (1,234,567), Indian (12,34,567) or East Asian (123,4567).
  • Safeguards. Division by zero, overflow, invalid factorials, impossible dates (31 Feb) and malformed lines show no result, so you never see a wrong number.
